Privacy Policy

version: 3.2 effective from: 16.11.2026 applies to: the Painless app

Painless is an app where you talk to an AI assistant about musculoskeletal pain, keep a diary and exercise according to a plan. Descriptions of your complaints, diary entries and plans are health data - we treat them with the utmost care. This policy explains in plain language what data we collect, why, who we entrust it to, how long we keep it and how you can delete it at any time.

This is the English version of the Privacy Policy. The Polish version has the same content.

04Artificial intelligence and automated processing

The assistant's answers, exercise plans and the conclusions in weekly and monthly summaries are created by an AI model from Anthropic. We send the model the content of the conversation and, for summaries, an overview of your entries (without notes). We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that would produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect you (Article 22 GDPR): the assistant gives guidance, and the decision is always yours. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, the data we send to the model is not used to train models. When choosing videos for exercises, the model receives only the name of the exercise and the titles and channels of the videos found, without any data about you.

08Storage on your device (cookies and similar)

Painless stores on your device only what is strictly necessary to provide the service you ask for:

  • sign-in session - so that you do not have to sign in every time you open the app;
  • exercise recordings - in the browser's storage (IndexedDB), because we do not keep them on our servers;
  • notification subscription - if you turn on reminders;
  • app settings - e.g. the state of the timer.

The analytics tool (PostHog) does not store anything on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

09Your rights - including deleting your account

You have the right to:

  • erasure of your data - the quickest way is to do it yourself: Settings → “Delete account and statistics”. This irreversibly deletes your account and all data in the app and cancels your Premium subscription. Billing data remains with the payment operator and in accounting for the period required by law (section 07);
  • access to your data and to receive a copy of it;
  • rectification (correction) of your data;
  • restriction of processing;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest, including analytics;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw your consent to the processing of health data at any time - this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. Without consent you cannot use the conversation with the assistant; if you have Premium, you can then end the contract under the rules in the Terms of Service;
  • lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland, uodo.gov.pl).

For matters the app itself does not handle, write to movepainless@gmail.com. We reply within one month at the latest.

We are not able to delete anonymous feedback at your request, because we do not know which feedback comes from you - that is what makes it anonymous.

10How we protect your data

  • The connection to the app is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each user technically has access only to their own data - access rules are enforced in the database.
  • The conversation with the assistant is only possible after the consents have been given - this block works on the server side.
  • We keep access keys to services on the server side only.
  • Passwordless sign-in (one-time code) removes the risk of password leaks.
  • The printout link contains a long, random key; we store only its hash in the database. The link expires after 14 days and you can switch it off at any time. Note: anyone who receives it can open it - send it only to someone you trust.
  • No health data is sent to the payment operator, the analytics tool or the notification services.
