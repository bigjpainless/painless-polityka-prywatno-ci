03What Painless is and what it is not

Painless is an educational app. In it, you talk to an assistant about musculoskeletal pain and receive general guidance: example exercises, tips on everyday habits and information on when it is worth seeing a specialist. You can also keep a diary of the discomfort you feel and how regularly you exercise.

Three things you need to know before you start:

  • You are talking to artificial intelligence, not to a human. Answers, plans and weekly and monthly summaries are created automatically and may contain errors or inaccuracies. No physiotherapist reads or replies to your messages.
  • Painless does not diagnose, does not provide treatment and does not recommend medicines. It does not replace a visit to a doctor or a physiotherapist. Summaries and the printout for your physiotherapist only bring together data you have recorded yourself, without any assessment of your health.
  • Do not make decisions about your health solely on the basis of what the app says. If your symptoms are sudden, getting worse or worrying you, contact a doctor. In a life-threatening situation, call 112.

If symptoms that require an urgent medical consultation come up in the conversation, the assistant will stop giving guidance and ask you to contact a specialist. Until the situation is clarified, the app then pauses exercise plans, summaries, the printout and reminders - also in Premium. This is not a formality, but the most important safety feature of this app (more in section 08).

Painless is not a medical device and is not intended for diagnosing, monitoring or treating any disease.
